## About

I'm a BS Computer Science graduate of the **University of the Philippines Visayas – Miagao** (2025), based in Iloilo, Philippines. I teach college **Software Engineering** and **Mathematics**, and I build full-stack web apps.

- **Teaching:** Software development, software architecture, and networking at Central Philippine University (2026); college mathematics at Iloilo Doctors' College (A.Y. 2025–2026).
- **Building:** Web systems with React, TypeScript, Node.js, PHP, MySQL, and Firebase.

## Featured project

### [Centivate](https://centivate.vercel.app) · campus facility complaint & maintenance system

Students and staff report broken facilities and follow each repair with a tracking code. Admins triage and assign work orders, technicians see only their own tasks, and Google Gemini suggests urgency and safety risks for staff to review. Built solo for a senior high school practical research project.
